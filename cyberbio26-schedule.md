  - S3b chair should not be Rami Puzis (co-author #33), Charles Frick (#2), or Mihai Pop/Tudor Dumitraș (co-authors #50).

Chairs should plan to run an open Q&A across the session's speakers at the end. All speakers are expected to remain for the full session.
